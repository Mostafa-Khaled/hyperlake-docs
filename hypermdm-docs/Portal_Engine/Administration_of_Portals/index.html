Administration of Portals

Experience portals powered by the portal engine are pretty much standard HyperMDM applications. The configuration takes place in HyperMDM documents. These define domain, structure and content of the portals.

In addition to the configuration itself, there are a couple of aspects that need to be considered when administrating portals.
